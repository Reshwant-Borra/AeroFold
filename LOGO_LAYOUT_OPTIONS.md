## Recommendation for Video Hero Background

**Since the hero has a full-screen video background, the best options are:**

### ⭐ **Option 2** (Top Left Overlay in Hero) - **RECOMMENDED**
- **Perfect for video backgrounds**: Positioned absolutely, doesn't block video content
- **Always visible**: Stays in view as user scrolls
- **Modern aesthetic**: Common pattern in modern web design
- **Non-intrusive**: Doesn't compete with video for attention
- **Works with transparent header**: Logo visible even when header is transparent

### **Option 1** (Centered in Hero with Content)
- **High impact**: Maximum brand visibility
- **Requires content overlay**: Need hero text/CTAs positioned to not block logo
- **Can work**: If hero content is positioned around the logo

### **Option 7** (Watermark/Background Element)
- **Very subtle**: Faded logo in background
- **Less brand recognition**: May be too subtle
- **Good for secondary branding**: Could combine with Option 2

**Implementation Recommendation:**
- **Primary**: Implement Option 2 (Top Left Overlay) - best balance of visibility and non-intrusiveness
- **Optional**: Add Option 7 (Watermark) as subtle secondary branding in video background
